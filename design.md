# Totus Tuus — design direction
Inspired by the supplied reference, not its copy: deep plum (#211b28), white type, electric mint (#00d7b6), tightly tracked oversized typography, asymmetric editorial sections, square portrait panels, fine rules and geometric accents. Orange appears once in a display headline.
Typography: Poppins body, Archivo Black display. Desktop max width 1280px with 64px gutters; mobile 22px. Generous 100–140px section spacing. Pill buttons and square content panels.
Actual supplied crest and portraits anchor the identity. Names derive from filenames; roles and bios remain pending. BioBuzz is verified 2026–2027 FIRST season artwork, not a sponsor. No stock pictures presented as team images.
Home: hero, about, member roster, season, gallery, awards, sponsor opportunities, FIRST resources, support and contact. Member and gallery dialogs are interactive. Missing contact, social and donation destinations show explicit pending notices, never fake submissions. No database or API additions for the static first version. Keep template providers and analytics.
Keyboard-accessible controls, visible focus, modal Escape/backdrop close, responsive menu, reduced-motion support.
